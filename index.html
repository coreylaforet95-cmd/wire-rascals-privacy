Privacy Policy

Wire Rascals Conduit Bending — Android

Last updated: November 17, 2026
Production package: ca.wirerascals.bending

The Android app works offline. It has no account system, advertising, analytics, or automatic project uploads. Your calculator inputs and saved projects are stored on your device.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy describes how Wire Rascals handles information in the Wire Rascals Conduit Bending Android app and when you contact us or visit this privacy page. It also describes the same offline features in the separate beta package, ca.wirerascals.bending.test. These packages keep separate app storage.

For privacy questions, contact Wire Rascals at wirerascals@gmail.com.

2. Information stored in the app

The app stores calculator inputs, unit and bender preferences, saved layouts, bend plans, project names and project details in its private Android WebView storage on your device. This lets you return to your work. Wire Rascals does not receive this information automatically or keep a server copy of your projects.

The current app does not request location, camera, microphone, or contacts permissions. It does not contain advertising or analytics SDKs, and it does not send usage events or crash reports automatically to Wire Rascals.

3. Files, exports and printing

You can choose a file to import using Android's document picker. You can export backups, CSV files and problem reports to a destination you choose, or print using Android's print service.

If you choose a cloud document provider, network printer or another service, that provider may receive the selected file or print content under its own privacy policy. Exported files remain outside the app until you delete them. Do not include confidential employer, customer, patient or other sensitive information without appropriate authorization.

The app's “Report a problem” feature prepares a report for you to copy or save. It does not automatically send the report to us.

4. Support messages

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, message, attachments and any other information you send. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue and manage support or privacy requests. Our email provider, Google, processes these messages to provide email services.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to resolve and follow up on the request, meet applicable legal obligations or handle a dispute. You may request access, correction or deletion by emailing us. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify that the request concerns your own correspondence.

5. Google Play purchases and platform services

Google Play handles the app's upfront purchase, payment information, delivery and applicable purchase or refund processes. The app does not collect payment-card details or offer in-app purchases. Google may make purchase/order records, reviews and app-quality information available to the developer through Play Console; we use information available there for purchase support, refunds and app maintenance, subject to Google's settings and retention rules.

Google Play, Android, Android System WebView and any platform purchase-protection services operate under their own privacy practices. This policy's statements about the offline app do not describe all information processed independently by Google or your device's operating system. See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. External websites and this privacy webpage

Manufacturer reference links open in your external browser. Those websites handle connection information and any information you provide under their own policies.

The public copy of this privacy page is hosted on Render. Loading it sends ordinary web connection information, such as your IP address, browser details and request time, to Render and its delivery/security infrastructure. This page contains no analytics, advertising, forms, tracking scripts or third-party fonts. Hosting infrastructure may retain technical information for operation and security under its own practices. See Render's Privacy Policy.

7. Sharing and sale

Wire Rascals does not sell or rent personal information or use it for targeted advertising. We do not automatically share your locally stored projects. Information you voluntarily send to support is handled by our email service provider and may be disclosed when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect legal rights. Processing by email, hosting and platform providers may take place outside your province or country, including in the United States.

8. Retention, deletion and security

Your app data stays in app storage until you delete it, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. Deleting an individual project does not necessarily remove saved calculator inputs or preferences; clear app storage to remove all local app data. Wire Rascals cannot remotely retrieve or delete your local projects.

Uninstalling or clearing app storage does not delete separately exported files, copies held by document providers, or print jobs. Delete those separately through the relevant provider. Keep backups of work you need: Wire Rascals does not provide automatic cloud backup or recovery.

The Android app disables Android's standard app-data backup setting. Device manufacturers and services you choose may have their own migration or backup features. No storage or transmission method is completely secure; protect access to your device and exported files.

9. Intended audience

Wire Rascals is intended for electricians and apprentices aged 16 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek their personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information to our support address.

10. Policy changes and contact

We will update this policy before introducing features that materially change how the app handles personal information. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Privacy and support: wirerascals@gmail.com.
